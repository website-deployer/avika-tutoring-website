ABOUT US

What we actually do

We give San Antonio students free, one-on-one support — so a tight budget never decides whether a kid keeps up.

Our Mission

Free academic support for underserved students, so every child in San Antonio has the momentum to keep up, catch up, and get ahead.

Who We Serve

Elementary and middle schoolers (grades K–8) across San Antonio — especially families who can't afford private tutoring.

Personalized Learning

We match each student with a tutor and a plan built around how they learn best — not a one-size-fits-all worksheet.
